### Project Description
An absurd, deliberately confusing, and rage-inducing volume control interface that completely eliminates traditional sliders in favor of three physics-driven, unpredictable interaction mechanics: **Tilt (Cup-Pour)**, **Ballistic Cannon**, and **Inverted Window Crank**. Built with custom HTML5 Canvas physics and an interactive Web Audio API procedural sound engine.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Traditional volume sliders are intolerably efficient. You click, you drag, and in 200 milliseconds your audio is set to an exact comfortable level. This robbed humanity of true suspense, frustration, and chaotic acoustic roulette. Why should adjusting your speaker volume be easier than landing an artillery shell?

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We engineered three infuriating alternative volume control methods that turn basic audio adjustment into an adrenaline-pumping ordeal:

1. **🧪 Method 1: The Tilt (Cup-Pour Method)**
   - A horizontal liquid chamber suspended on a fulcrum.
   - You must tilt the ends up and down like pouring from a beaker.
   - **Tilt Right** → Liquid pours into the chamber from a supply reservoir (+flow rate).
   - **Tilt Left** → Liquid drains out through the nozzle into the waste basin (-flow rate).
   - Includes realistic inertia waves, bubble buoyancy, and elastic spring wobbles when you let go.

2. **💥 Method 2: The Ballistic Cannon Method**
   - A mini 2D artillery cannon mounted to the left of a volume bar runway (0% to 100%).
   - You aim the cannon barrel with the mouse and click-and-hold to charge power.
   - Releasing fires a cannonball through a parabolic ballistic arc.
   - Wherever the cannonball crashes on the runway directly sets your volume!
   - **Miss Penalties**: Undershooting into the dirt slams volume to **0%**; overshooting into the stratosphere blasts it to **100% EAR-DESTROYER**.

3. **⚙️ Method 3: The Inverted Window Crank Method**
   - A 2D mechanical window crank positioned on the right side of the volume bar.
   - Requires mouse-driven circular rotation around the crank spindle.
   - **The Inversion**: Rolling **Left (Counter-Clockwise)** increases volume; rolling **Right (Clockwise)** decreases volume (exact opposite of human instinct).
   - **Gear Stripping**: Cranking too aggressively strips the mechanical gears, screeching and dropping volume by -5%!

## Project Features & Mechanics

- **🎛️ Master Volume HUD**: Real-time synchronized master percentage (0% to 100%), decibel conversion (-inf dB to 0.0 dB), and dynamic rage statuses (*"Quiet Murmur"*, *"Painfully Moderate"*, *"Neighbor Disturber"*, *"Sonic Apocalypse"*).
- **🎶 Built-in Web Audio Synthesizer**: Procedural background music engine with 4 selectable soundtracks (Chiptune, Elevator Muzak, Retro Synthwave, Pure Panic) directly modulated by the master volume.
- **🎯 The Impossible Calibration Challenge**: Generates random target volumes (e.g., *"Set volume to 43% ± 1.5% and hold for 1.0s"*), tracking attempts and escalating the Frustration Index.
- **🔥 Chaos Arena Mode**: A unified testing dashboard showcasing all three mechanisms simultaneously.
- **⚡ Earthquake Jitter Mode**: Toggleable unstable physics setting introducing seismic tremors to your controls.
